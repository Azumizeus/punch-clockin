JUDGE GUIDE — 15 MINUTES — REAL DEVNET

How to test PUNCH · step by step

Everything money-related in the app is a real signed devnet transaction. Nothing is simulated.
1 · Download the signed APK (arm64)
  1. Install the APK on the Solana Seeker (or any Android with an MWA wallet on devnet). No account, no signup.
  2. Open the app → “Ouvrir mon portefeuille”. The system Seed Vault sheet asks for a real authorization on devnet.
  3. A welcome grant arrives from the treasury within seconds if your wallet is empty: 0.05 SOL + 5 USDC + 20 USDT + 5,000 SKR. Check your address on the explorer — it's a real transfer.
  4. Tap the bolt (CLOCK IN). Seed Vault asks for your signature — a real memo transaction. The paper ticket shows time, country, the 92/3/5 rule and an on-chain stamp (clickable signature).
  5. Try the money features — each one asks for a real signature: Wallet → Swap SKR⇄USDC · Stake/Unstake (honest 1.5% fee) · Board missions · Say hi to a nearby Seeker (0.10 USDC from the treasury).
  6. Leave the network (Settings): a real signed transaction that honestly decrements the shared counters.
What to verify as a judge: every action triggers the Seed Vault prompt · the treasury address FUiCbnDhEEJtz9Zcj66hGB54iGMGeyjRKD7CsTwpihJn moves on explorer.solana.com (cluster=devnet) · every receipt's signature is clickable · failures show the exact reason and change nothing.
