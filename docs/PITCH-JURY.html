PUNCH

Why this app? For whom? What does it do?

Why this app?

Most "proof of presence" apps on crypto rails are fake in one of two ways: either presence isn't verified, or the money split is hidden behind a token model nobody can audit. PUNCH fixes both. Presence is proven by a real Solana transaction signed by a real Seeker phone through Seed Vault. Every payment follows one printed rule: 92% to the person who showed up, 3% to SKR holders, 5% to the app. No hidden fee.

What does it do?

Tap once a day, a real devnet transaction records your presence, and it unlocks small paid jobs nearby — done in minutes, paid in USDC/USDT. Pay someone else to do a job, swap USDC/USDT/SKR, stake SKR for earlier access to better jobs, watch a live globe of Seekers punching in worldwide. Every money action is a real signed Solana transaction — nothing fake in local storage.

Who is it for?

Seeker phone owners who want their daily presence to mean something economically — gig workers, community organizers verifying attendance, or anyone who wants a small honest income without trusting a black-box algorithm. Also the SKR ecosystem itself: staking gives real utility without printing new supply.

Why is it fun?

The ticket you get after punching in looks like a real paper receipt — timestamp, country, the exact cut printed on it, an on-chain stamp. Saying hi to a nearby Seeker pays both of you ten cents from the app's own cut. The globe spins with real drag physics. A small, honest economy you can watch move in real time.

Who does it target?

Solana Mobile Seeker owners at launch, expanding to any Mobile Wallet Adapter–compatible device. Scales from a hackathon demo (devnet) to a real presence-and-micro-gig economy on mainnet.
