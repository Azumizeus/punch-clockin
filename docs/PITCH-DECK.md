## English — 10 slides

**Slide 1 — Title**
PUNCH — Proof of presence. Paid honestly.
You show up. You get paid. 92 / 3 / 5.
Built for CLOCK IN · Solana Mobile · devnet demo
*(visual: app icon + CLOCK IN screen)*

**Slide 2 — The problem**
Presence apps on crypto are fake in one of two ways:
- the check-in isn't verified (anyone can fake it from a browser),
- or the money split is hidden behind a token model nobody can audit.
Gig workers and communities have no honest way to turn "I was here" into income.

**Slide 3 — The solution**
One tap a day on a real Seeker phone → a real signed Solana transaction proves you're here → it unlocks small paid gigs nearby.
Every payment follows one printed rule: **92% worker · 3% SKR holders · 5% app**. Visible on every receipt. Never hidden.

**Slide 4 — How it works (product)**
- **Punch in** → memo transaction signed in Seed Vault, confirmed on devnet in seconds.
- **Ticket** → paper-style receipt: time, country, 92/3/5, on-chain stamp.
- **Gigs** → confirm a place is open, leave an honest review, scan a code — paid in USDC/USDT.
- **Post a job** → lock real funds from your wallet; the treasury pays the worker.

**Slide 5 — The SKR economy**
SKR staking gives **earlier access to better-paid jobs** — real utility, zero new supply.
Swap USDC/USDT/SKR at a fixed rate; unstake carries an honest 1.5% protocol fee funding the protocol and SKR holders.
*(This is our CLOCK IN SKR-integration entry.)*

**Slide 6 — Everything is real**
Punch in, get paid, swap, stake, say-hi, leave the network: **every single one is a real signed devnet transaction** through Mobile Wallet Adapter / Seed Vault.
Treasury is public: `FUiCbnDhEEJtz9Zcj66hGB54iGMGeyjRKD7CsTwpihJn` — watch balances move on the explorer while you use the app.
No fake numbers in local storage. Anywhere.

**Slide 7 — UX & delight**
- A real drag-physics globe: every punch in the world drops a live dot.
- "Say hi" to a nearby Seeker pays you both $0.10 — a social gesture turned into a real micro-transaction.
- 3 skins × 4 themes (incl. Solana Seeker Gold), idle mirror clock screen, interactive guided tour.
Built native (Expo/React Native) — signed APK, no Expo Go needed for judges.

**Slide 8 — Market & stickiness**
- Launch: Solana Mobile Seeker owners (Seed Vault built in, no seed phrases).
- Daily ritual: punch in → get gigs → get paid → come back tomorrow. Staking locks users in.
- Expansion: any Mobile Wallet Adapter device, then community attendance (events, coworking, crews).

**Slide 9 — Roadmap**
